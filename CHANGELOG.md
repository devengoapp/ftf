## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-10

### Changed

- **Breaking:** requires Ruby 3.2 or later ([#11](https://github.com/devengoapp/ftf/pull/11)).
- Releases are published with trusted publishing and a Sigstore attestation ([#12](https://github.com/devengoapp/ftf/pull/12)).

### Dependencies

- Drops the unused `fiddle` and `rdoc` runtime dependencies, and relaxes `zeitwerk` to `~> 2.6` ([#11](https://github.com/devengoapp/ftf/pull/11)).
